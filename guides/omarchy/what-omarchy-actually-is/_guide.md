# What Omarchy Actually Is

Someone told you to look at Omarchy, or you saw a screenshot of a gorgeous desktop with no dock, no icons, and windows tiled edge to edge. Then you read "Arch, Hyprland, Quickshell" and every word was new. Before you wipe a drive, you want one thing: a clear picture of what this is and whether it is for you.

This guide gives you that picture. It names the layers, explains the four ways Omarchy will feel different from what you know, and helps you decide, with a safe way to try it. Checked against Omarchy 4.0.4.

## Prerequisite

None. If the word "Linux" itself still feels fuzzy, [Linux From Zero](/guides/linux-from-zero) is a gentle companion, but you can start here without it.
